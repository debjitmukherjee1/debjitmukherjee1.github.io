02 · Method

How a rating gets set.

Every model carries several valuation methods and a stated blend. Never a lone DCF.

  1. ScreenCoverage starts from a specific question (a re-rating, a margin debate, a capex cycle), checked against filings before a model is opened.
  2. ModelIncome statement, balance sheet and cash flow built from the annual report, then the valuation that fits the business: DCF, excess return for banks, sum of the parts for conglomerates.
  3. ValueSeveral methods, a stated blend, sensitivities and bull and bear cases. The gap between methods is explained, not averaged away.
  4. PublishA rated note with the Excel model attached, logged on a public track record that keeps the losers.
