Gross spread = (offer + dividends expected before close − price) ÷ price. Annualised to the expected close. Implied odds (risk-neutral) = (price × (1 + T-bill yield × days ÷ 365) − break price) ÷ (offer + dividends − break price), using the 13-week T-bill; the break price is the unaffected price moved with the S&P 500 since, a desk assumption.
